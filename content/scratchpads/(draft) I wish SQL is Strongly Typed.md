
Alternative title: I hate temporal data types


I work a lot with temporal data, and you know that developers like to have "just-in-case" for everything. In this case, a temporal data type. 
Not all business events need microsecond resolution for their date-time. Some, if not most of a typical standard business data  are completely okay to only store in date precision. I think you know where this is going.
